## Method and constraints

- ZERO FABRICATION, ZERO SPECULATION. Every award/contract below comes from a real public source cited with a verbatim URL.
- Donation facts come from the SBI bond-level data already ingested in this project (purchase totals from `data/parsed_sbi_purchase.csv`; party-wise encashment from the bond-number join, `ingestion_ledger` id 4, read live from Neon on 2026-09-23). No database writes were made for this report.
- Party names are verbatim from the SBI redemption data. Some are truncated in the source (e.g. `DRAVIDA MUNNETRA KAZHAGAM (D...`).
- Donations and any government awards are listed as **separate facts with their own dates**. This report does **not** imply that any award was caused by any donation, or vice versa. No causal claim is made anywhere.
- `NOT AVAILABLE IN SOURCE DATA` means the searches performed for this pilot found no citable public record of a government tender/contract award for that company — not that none exists.

- No causal claim is made linking any donation to any award. Dates are given separately for each fact.
