## Method notes

- Listed companies (Vedanta, Bharti Airtel): own audited results releases / BSE filings — strongest tier.
- Subsidiaries of listed parents (Haldia Energy, Dhariwal Infrastructure via CESC; Utkal Alumina via Hindalco): parent-published investor materials and annual reports — primary company-published sources for subsidiary performance.
- Private companies with their own annual reports (Essel Mining, via its 75th Annual Report): company document, aggregator-hosted copy — flagged as such.
- Group-company prospectus filings (Madanlal via Keventer Agro DRHP; MKJ via DRHP citations as corroboration): SEBI-filed primary documents.
- Credit-rating rationales (Birla Carbon via ICRA; Dhariwal via CARE): free public primary-grade sources publishing audited figures; not MCA filings.
- Registry aggregators (ZaubaCorp/Tofler/TheCompanyCheck/InstaFinancials/EMIS/MiraInform/edgeinsights) appear ONLY in Notes as corroboration and were never used as primary revenue/profit sources. Aggregator figures lack consistent financial-year attribution and disagree across sources (e.g. Future Gaming ₹22,966 cr vs ">₹5000 cr" band), consistent with the MCA pilot's warning.
- No paid MCA filings, no account creation, no CAPTCHA attempts were made. Audited standalone figures for the 10 unresolvable private companies sit behind paid MCA AOC-4 access — a budget decision for the full run.

## Source-access caveats

- Essel Mining's annual report was read in full but via an aggregator-hosted copy (wwipl.com); the document itself is the company's own 75th Annual Report. A browser-capable session may verify against esselmining.com.
- Jindal Steel's FY24 figures come from secondary analyses with a revenue-definition conflict (₹42,095.3 cr Net Sales vs ₹50,026.76 cr Sales); the company's own annual report or BSE/NSE full-year filing should be sourced to settle it.
- DLF Commercial Developers is amalgamated; only a share-of-consolidated-profit figure exists in public sources — no standalone revenue/PAT.
- Yashoda and Chennai Green Woods remain identity-unresolved; no financials were attributed to either.
