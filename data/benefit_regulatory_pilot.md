# Regulatory approvals/licenses pilot — top 20 SBI electoral-bond purchasers

**Retrieval date for every record below: 2026-09-23.**
**Scope:** top 20 corporate purchasers by bond value in the SBI purchaser list. Company names below are reproduced exactly as they appear in the SBI data.

## Methodology — what counts as a regulatory approval

A qualifying record is a **dated, affirmative act by a public authority involving the identified legal entity**, such as:

- a licence or licence authorization granted to the entity;
- an environmental / forest clearance or pollution-control consent granted to the entity;
- a mining lease, letter of intent, or preferred-bidder decision issued to the entity;
- a statutory project approval or official industrial-investment / high-level clearance naming the entity;
- another formal permission required by the entity to operate or expand.

**Not counted:** applications, proposals, recommendations not yet approved, pending requests, routine incorporation, private certifications, accreditations whose entity or date could not be verified, contracts with no public regulatory decision behind them, and general policy changes not specifically involving the company.

**Identity rule:** the approval must involve the SBI purchaser as the identified legal entity. Ambiguous or similarly named entities were left unresolved. Where a record exists but the legal-entity identity could not be independently confirmed, the record is reported **with an explicit caveat** and is not asserted as the purchaser's.

**Anti-inference rule:** every approval below and every electoral-bond purchase are separate dated facts. No approval, licence, clearance, contract, or policy decision below is claimed to have caused, rewarded, or been caused by any bond purchase, and no bond purchase is claimed to have caused any approval.

## Record-by-record provenance notes

1. **FUTURE GAMING AND HOTEL SERVICES PR** — The writ petitioner in the cited court judgment is "Future Gaming And Hotel Services Pvt. Ltd." The judgment records that the Government of Arunachal Pradesh issued an RFP on 01.11.2013 for distributors/selling agents of Arunachal Pradesh State Lottery tickets; the petitioner was appointed on 23.06.2014 as distributor for the paper lottery set; and on 27.07.2015 an agreement was executed between the Secretary, Department of State Lottery, Government of Arunachal Pradesh and the petitioner, giving it sole distributorship of the conventional paper lottery. This is a formal state-government authorization to market state lottery tickets under the Lotteries (Regulation) framework. The 2015-07-27 agreement date is used because it is the formal written authorization; the appointment was recorded on 2014-06-23.

2. **HALDIA ENERGY LIMITED** — The project brief summary hosted on the official EC portal (environmentclearance.nic.in) states: "Environmental Clearance for the existing project was accorded on 01.10.2008 and subsequently transferred in the name of Haldia Energy Limited on 18.01.2010." The 2010-01-18 transfer is the entity-specific affirmative act; the 2008-10-01 clearance predates the company's holding and is not listed as its own approval.

3. **VEDANTA LIMITED** — Vedanta Limited's own stock-exchange filing (Ref. VEDL/Sec./SE/19-20/18, dated 30 April 2019, hosted on vedantalimited.com) confirms that MoEF&CC "accorded an environmental clearance" to the company's Oil and Gas Division for the RJ-ON-90/1 expansion. A projects report dated 26 April 2019 cites an official circular stating the Union Environment Ministry gave final clearance and the EC certificate had been issued. The precise letter date is not stated in either source, so only the month (2019-04) is given, with this caveat.

4. **ESSEL MINING AND INDS LTD** — The cited document is Essel Mining & Industries Limited's own authorization letter (dated 18 April 2020) addressed to the MP Forest Department's Nodal Officer, hosted on the MoEF&CC forest-clearance portal (forestsclearance.nic.in). It states the company "has been declared as Preferred Bidder for grant of Mining Lease over 364 Ha area of Bunder Diamond Block in Chhatarpur District of Madhya Pradesh and Letter of Intent (LOI) in this regard has been issued by the Government of Madhya Pradesh on 19th December 2019." Provenance is therefore a company statement on a government portal, not the LoI itself; reported exactly as stated.

5. **WESTERN UP POWER TRANSMISSION COMPANY LI MITED** — The UPERC licence document itself (hosted on uperc.org) is addressed to "WESTERN U P POWER TRANSMISSION COMPANY LTD. (WUPPTCL) (License no.1 of 2012)", conferring a transmission licence under Section 14 of the Electricity Act, 2003 for 25 years, "Dated: 20th June, 2012", Place: Lucknow. Entity name matches the SBI purchaser.

6. **BHARTI AIRTEL LIMITED** — The specialist telecom report (OnlyTech) reproduces DoT licence data: licence no. 20-1218/2020-AS-I, applicant company "BHARTI AIRTEL LIMITED", type "VNO", issue date and sign date both 21-10-2021, services "ISP A, NLD, ILD, VSAT", service area "National Area". This is a secondary source explicitly reproducing the Department of Telecommunications licence register entry.

7. **UTKAL ALUMINA INTERNATIONAL LIMITED** — The Hindalco-hosted half-yearly EC compliance statement for Utkal Alumina (Oct 2022–Mar 2023) lists the EC as: "Environmental Clearance for Expansion of Alumina Refinery Plant (1.5 MTPA to 3.0 MTPA) and Cogeneration Plant (90 MW to 150 MW) by M/s Utkal Alumina International Ltd. at Rayagada District, Odisha [F. No. J-11011/753/2007-IA.II(I) dated 25.06.2018]". This is a company-published compliance filing citing the ministry letter; the underlying MoEF&CC letter itself was not independently retrievable on 2026-09-23.

8. **JINDAL STEEL AND POWER LIMITED** — The Hindu BusinessLine report (updated 7 July 2021) states, citing an Odisha government statement, that the State High-Level Clearance Authority headed by Chief Minister Naveen Patnaik "on Tuesday" approved the expansion of the JSPL Angul plant to 25.2 MTPA against ₹24,652 crore investment. The referenced Tuesday is 2021-07-06 (verified: 6 July 2021 was a Tuesday). The decision date 2021-07-06 is used with the reporting caveat shown in the table.

9. **DHARIWAL INFRASTRUCTURE LIMITED** — The company's half-yearly EC compliance report for 1 Oct 2021–31 Mar 2022 (dated 25/05/2022, submitted to the MoEFCC Regional Office, Nagpur, and hosted on dilenergy.co.in) states: "Dhariwal Infrastructure Limited has been granted Environmental Clearance for 2 x 300 MW Thermal Power Plant vide MoEF&CC EC No. J-13011/10/2009-IA. II (T) dated 04-12-2009." Company-submitted regulatory filing hosted on the company site; the underlying ministry letter was not separately retrieved.

10. **RUNGTA SONS P LTD** — The MoEF letter text hosted on the official EC portal (environmentclearance.nic.in) "accords environmental clearance … to the above mentioned proposal of M/s Rungta Sons (P) Ltd." for enhancement of iron ore production from 1.65 MTPA to 4.5 MTPA (2.85 MTPA ROM + 1.65 MTPA low-grade ore) with a 1.44 MTPA wet beneficiation plant. The order number (J-11015/206/2012-IA.II(M)) and date (19.11.2013) are taken from the project feasibility report quoting that order. Entity name matches the SBI purchaser exactly.

11. **BIRLACARBONINDIAPRIVATELIMITED** — **Identity caveat (see below).** The MoEF letter (F.No. J-11011/350/2010-IA II (I), dated 21 September 2011), hosted on birlacarbon.com, "hereby accords environmental clearance" for expansion of the Carbon Black Plant (12,500 to 18,750 MTPA) with power plant (33.7 to 47 MW) at K-16, Phase-II, SIPCOT, Gummidipoondi, Tamil Nadu. **The letter is addressed to "M/s Hi-Tech Carbon, India (A Unit of Aditya Birla NUVO Limited)"**, not to "Birla Carbon India Private Limited". Legal-entity continuity between the addressee and the SBI purchaser could not be independently confirmed from public records on 2026-09-23. The record is reported because it is a genuine ministry letter hosted on the company's own website concerning the Gummidipoondi carbon-black plant, but it is **not asserted** to be the SBI purchaser's own approval.

## Limitations

- Secondary sources (news reports, specialist telecom reporting, company-published compliance statements, court-judgment records) are used where the underlying government letter or register was not directly retrievable; each such case is flagged above with its exact provenance.
- "NOT AVAILABLE IN SOURCE DATA" means no qualifying record was found in public sources on 2026-09-23; it does not mean no approval exists.
- Approvals and electoral-bond purchases are presented as separate dated facts only. This pilot makes no claim — and none should be inferred — that any approval listed here was caused by, rewarded, or otherwise linked to any bond purchase, or vice versa.
- No data was written to the database for this pilot.
