                                     I.    Share of Corporate & Individual Donors who purchased Electoral Bonds,
                                                          12 April 2019 to 15 February 2024
Out of the total 18,871 bonds purchased between 12.04.2019 and 15.02.2024, 16,631 were purchased by corporate/business houses and 2,240 bonds were
purchased by individuals.

During this period, corporate/business houses purchased a total amount of Electoral Bonds worth Rs 11,780.0297 cr (96.91%) while individuals donated
Electoral Bonds worth Rs 375.4835 cr (3.09%). Together this amounted to 18,871 bonds worth Rs 12,155.5132 cr.

                               II.        Top 25 corporate donors who contributed through Electoral Bonds between
                                                          12 April 2019 to 15 February 2024


The top 25 donors of Electoral Bonds to political parties include all corporates and business houses only. Future Gaming & Hotel Services
Private Limited purchased the highest amount of Electoral Bonds worth Rs 1368 cr having the maximum share of 11.254%. This is followed by
Megha Engineering & Infrastructures Limited which purchased 966 bonds worth Rs 966 cr (having a share of 7.947%).


Together the top 25 donors contributed 6658 bonds worth Rs 6312.67 cr (51.93%) to political parties between 12 April 2019 and 15 February
2024.
