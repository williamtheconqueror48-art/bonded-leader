                                  Party-wise Encashment details of Electoral Bonds between 12 April 2019 to 15 February 2024
        Between 12 April 2019 and 15 February 2024, 20,421 number of bonds worth a total of Rs 12,769.0893 cr were encashed by the political parties.

        BJP received the highest electoral bonds (8633) worth Rs 6060.5111 cr (47.462%), followed by the AITC which received 3305 electoral bonds worth Rs
        1609.5314 cr (12.605%) and INC received 3146 bonds of Rs 1421.8655 cr (11.135%)

        Note: Names of all parties are exactly as per the data uploaded by the ECI on its website. Shiv Sena data has not been combined as it is not clear if it relates
        to Shiv Sena (Uddhav Balasaheb Thackrey) or Shiv Sena.
